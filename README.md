# YouTube Declutter

A browser extension that lets you hide distracting parts of YouTube. Choose exactly what you want gone — comments, Shorts, ads, buttons, the logo, and more.

## Features

- **Comments** – Hide the comment section under videos and the comments panel on Shorts.
- **Shorts** – Remove Shorts from your feed, sidebar, search results, and the Shorts player page.
- **Game Ads & Playables** – Block video masthead ads, ad slots, and the Playables game shelf.
- **Notifications** – Hide the notification bell button in the top bar.
- **Create Button** – Remove the Create (camera) button.
- **Join Button** – Hide membership join buttons.
- **Voice Search** – Disable the microphone button in the search bar.
- **YouTube Logo** – Hide the YouTube logo in the top‑left corner.
- **Category Chips** – Remove the filter chips row (All, Gaming, Music, etc.) on the homepage.
- **Top News Section** – Hide the "Top news" and "Breaking news" sections on the homepage.
- **AI Search (Ask YouTube)** – Remove the experimental "Ask" button in the search bar.

Each toggle works independently — turn things on or off whenever you want, with instant changes (no page reload needed).
